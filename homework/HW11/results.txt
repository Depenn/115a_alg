Task 5 - Riemann integral(f, low, high), f = x1^2+...+xn^2, low=0, high=1
==============================================================================
exact value = n/3.  Midpoint rule with k steps gives exactly n(1/3 - 1/(12k^2)).
  n | steps k |  cells k^n |     integral |     exact |    error |    time
  1 | 2000000 |  2,000,000 |   0.33333333 |   0.33333 |  2.1e-14 |  0.084s
  2 |    1414 |  1,999,396 |   0.66666658 |   0.66667 |  8.3e-08 |  0.178s
  5 |      18 |  1,889,568 |   1.66538066 |   1.66667 |  1.3e-03 |  0.259s
100 |       1 |          1 |  25.00000000 |  33.33333 |  8.3e+00 |  0.000s

5-D: the cost explodes as k^5
  k =  2:        32 cells, integral = 1.562500, error = 1.0e-01, 0.000s
  k =  4:     1,024 cells, integral = 1.640625, error = 2.6e-02, 0.000s
  k =  8:    32,768 cells, integral = 1.660156, error = 6.5e-03, 0.004s
  k = 16: 1,048,576 cells, integral = 1.665039, error = 1.6e-03, 0.137s
  k = 18: 1,889,568 cells, integral = 1.665381, error = 1.3e-03, 0.244s

100-D: with 2,000,000 cells allowed, only k = 1 fits (one cell, the centre).
       k = 1 gives 100 * 0.5^2 = 25, far from 33.333.
  steps=2: ValueError: 2^100 = 1.268e+30 cells is more than max_points=2,000,000; the Riemann sum is not feasible (curse of dimensionality)
  steps=10: ValueError: 10^100 = 1.000e+100 cells is more than max_points=2,000,000; the Riemann sum is not feasible (curse of dimensionality)
  (2^100 cells at 10^9 cells/s would take about 4 * 10^13 years.)

Because this f is a sum of one-variable terms, integral_separable gets the
same Riemann sum that the full k^100 grid would give, with only 100*k work:
  k =    1: (virtual 1^100 cells) integral = 25.00000000, error = 8.3e+00
  k =   10: (virtual 10^100 cells) integral = 33.25000000, error = 8.3e-02
  k =  100: (virtual 100^100 cells) integral = 33.33250000, error = 8.3e-04
  k = 1000: (virtual 1000^100 cells) integral = 33.33332500, error = 8.3e-06
